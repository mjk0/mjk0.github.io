    How to play

    Auction 45s is a partnership trick-taking game (white vs red). First team to 125 wins the series. Empty seats become robots.

    1. Choose a display name and enter the lobby.
    2. Sit at an open public seat, or Create private and invite friends.
    3. Press Start when you are ready. First dealer sits White.
    4. Bid (or pass). The high bidder sees the kitty, names trump, and keeps up to five cards.
    5. Everyone discards, fills back to five, then plays five tricks.
    6. Make your bid or go set. Click past-trick piles to review. Tables can opt in to Replay this deal (same hands, bidding starts over).

    Full rules and card ranks are linked in the header. After trump is named, a rank strip sits above your hand ( gear menu toggles it).
