Rules

This page borrows heavily from jord.ca/45s.html, with permission.

Players

Four players in two partnerships. Same-color seats are a team: white vs red. On the table, partners sit opposite. First team to 125 wins the series.

Deal

Each player gets five cards (a packet of three, then two). Four more cards form the kitty, which only the winning bidder will see. Deal goes clockwise from the dealer’s left: three around, two to the kitty, two around, two to the kitty.

The auction

Each trick is worth 5 points except the trick that takes the best card in play, which is worth 10. Five cards each means 30 points in a hand.

Bidding starts at the dealer’s left. Bids are 15, 20, 25, or 30 (or 60 if that team is in the hole). You may pass or raise. Once you pass, you are out of this auction.

The dealer may hold — match the high bid rather than raise. After a hold, the previous high bidder and the dealer may keep raising until one gives up.

Example: Player 1 bids 15. Player 2 passes. Player 3 bids 20. Dealer holds. Player 3 bids 25. Dealer declines 25, so Player 3 has it at 25.

The high bidder looks through the kitty, then names trump to complete the contract. A bid of 30 is “30 for 60”: make all five tricks and score 60; go set and lose only 30. A team below 0 may bid 60 for 120.

Discarding

After trump is named, each player may discard any number of cards (often all non-trump). The dealer then fills every hand back to five, no more than three cards at a time.

Card rank

Trump order is always 5, J, Ace of hearts, Ace, K, Q, then the small cards. Small cards run high-to-low in red, low-to-high in black (“highest in red, lowest in black”). Off-trump, the faces are K, Q, J, then the same small-card order. Ace of diamonds is the lowest off-trump diamond. Ace of hearts is always the third-best trump, even when hearts is not the named suit.

Play

The player to the left of the declarer leads. Play is clockwise. Highest card of the led suit wins unless trump is played; trump beats everything. If no trump is played, the led suit is the one that counts.

If trump is led, you must follow trump if you have any — except the 5 of trump may renege against any other trump. If a non-trump is led, you need not follow; any card is legal.

The winner of a trick leads the next.

Scoring

If the declaring team takes at least their bid (and at least 30 if they bid 30/60), they score the bid, or double if they made 30 or 60. If they fail, they are set: the bid is subtracted from their score, even if they took some points.

The other team scores the points they actually took, unless they are already at 100 or more and never bid this hand — then they score 0.

If everyone passes, the same dealer deals again; no score.

First team to 125 wins.
