Help

Short notes for this site. Card play itself is on the rules page.

Sign in

Choose any display name. Email is optional; if you add one, that name is locked to you on this server. This browser remembers recent names so you can switch or continue in one click. Signing in from a second tab or device takes over the first connection.

Lobby

Sit at any empty seat on a public table. Empty seats become robots when someone presses Start. Public tables are created as needed, so there is usually one waiting.

To play with friends, Create private. Only you, people you invite, and anyone already seated can see that table. Open Invite on your table to pick someone online, type a name, or ★ them as a friend (friends are auto-invited next time). Same-color seats are partners: white vs red. First dealer of a new series sits White.

The table

Bidding, then the high bidder sees the kitty, names trump, and keeps up to five cards. Everyone else discards; hands fill back to five; then five tricks. First team to 125 wins the series.

Lobby (header) opens a side panel without leaving your seat: chat, players, news/feedback, and — if you own a private table — Invite (works after Start too). Wide windows dock the panel beside the felt so play stays live; on a phone it slides over the table (Escape or ✕ closes). It is not the full lobby: create, sit, and start stay on the lobby page.

Name trump — four suit buttons sit just above your hand. Click one and the five highest trump (including the Ace of hearts) stay in your hand; the rest lift into a discard tray. Click a hand card to throw it, or a tray card to take it back. Changing the suit starts the split over. At most five cards stay in the hand.

Past tricks — click a pile to review that trick. Pile orientation follows the team that won it (white horizontal, red vertical); piles share a grey-blue back. Cards in a trick overlap in play order; the lead is the card at the bottom of the stack.

Scores — the header chips are the running series totals (White / Red). Click either chip for the hand-by-hand table; green marks the winner of each hand. When a new game starts, earlier series collapse to their finals.

Replay this deal — restores the same hands and kitty; bidding starts over. Series score from earlier hands is unchanged.

Rank strip — after trump is named, a rank strip sits above your hand. It always shows trump order; if a non-trump is led, a second row shows that suit. When it is your lead, hovering a non-trump card in your hand previews that suit in the second row (mouse only; a tap plays the card). Gold marks the current winner; blue marks your cards that can beat it. Hide with ×; the gear in the header opens a menu to turn it back on.

Leave table returns you to the lobby. You can sit again, or rejoin a hand that is still going.

If someone disconnects

The seat pauses. Others can hand it to a robot with Take over. Refreshing the table page puts you back in your seat.
