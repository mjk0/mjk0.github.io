    How to play

    Yoyo is a multiplayer variant of Asshole (President). Win by playing all of your cards before everyone else. Each game is a single hand—quick rounds. Keep playing to improve your average score.

    1. Choose a display name and enter the lobby.
    2. Sit at an open seat, or Create private to practice with bots.
    3. When everyone you want is seated, press Start (for private tables, only the host may start).
    4. On your turn, select a legal play and press Play, or Pass.
    5. Empty your hand first to win that game.

    Bots fill empty seats so tables can start without a full human roster.
